Purpose and authorized use

The current ParentEye app allows a person to share their Android device screen with a browser through a temporary link or QR code. ParentEye is intended for lawful parental supervision of a child's device with the parent or guardian's authority, appropriate notice to the child and any consent required by law. Do not use it for covert surveillance, monitoring a spouse or other adult, or access to a device you do not control.

How sharing works

The device user must explicitly start a session and approve Android's screen-capture prompt. Sharing continues through a visible foreground service until stopped, including when the screen is locked. A viewer can use the link from the same Wi-Fi/hotspot, or from anywhere when internet mode is selected and available. Keep the session link private: a person who gets a working link may be able to view the screen. Stop sharing to end access. The app does not provide audio or remote control.

Advertising

The app may show a home banner, an app-open ad, and an interstitial when the user starts a sharing session if an ad is ready. High picture quality can be unlocked for a session after the user chooses and completes a rewarded ad; Low and Medium remain available without it. An unavailable ad may mean High quality cannot be unlocked at that moment. Advertising and consent handling are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Availability and responsibility

Screen sharing requires a compatible Android device, granted capture permission and an available local network or internet relay. Connectivity, operating-system settings and third-party services can interrupt it. You are responsible for using the app lawfully, protecting the link and respecting other people's privacy. To the extent permitted by law, we do not guarantee uninterrupted service. These terms do not remove rights that cannot legally be excluded.
