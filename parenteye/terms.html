Purpose and permitted use

ParentEye is for a parent or legal guardian to supervise their own minor child's device. Use it only where you have the authority and any legally required notice and consent. Do not use it for hidden surveillance, monitoring a spouse or other adult, unauthorized access, or unlawful recording.

Accounts and pairing

You are responsible for your account, password, device PIN, and the devices you pair. Keep credentials private and remove devices you no longer manage. Pairing and live viewing require the app, a compatible device, permissions and network access. Do not interfere with another person's account or device.

Live screen viewing

A parent can request live viewing of a paired child device. The child app presents monitoring information and sharing status. Use live viewing only for legitimate parental supervision. You must follow local rules on children's privacy and screen content. Do not attempt to defeat disclosures, permissions or operating-system notifications.

The app may display advertising. The first paired device is free under the current product flow. Additional device slots may require the parent to choose and complete a rewarded advertisement. Ads can fail to load; an unavailable ad does not guarantee an additional slot. Other ad placements may include parent home and activity banners, a child home banner, an eligible parent app-open ad, and an interstitial after the parent ends a live session. The app does not charge the parent through the rewarded-ad flow.
