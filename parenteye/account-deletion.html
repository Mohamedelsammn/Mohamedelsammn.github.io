Data and account deletion

ParentEye (com.parenteye.app) · 2creative.apps · Updated 20 November 2026

English

Current Android version

The current ParentEye screen-sharing app does not create a ParentEye user account. It stores app preferences locally on the device. To end a live session, use Stop sharing in the app or its ongoing notification. To remove local settings, open Android Settings → Apps → ParentEye → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This does not erase data independently held by advertising or network providers; see the Privacy Policy for those providers and their controls.

Earlier ParentEye accounts

If you used an earlier ParentEye version that created a parent or child account, you can request deletion of that legacy account and associated data without using the old app. Email dr.abdellrahman2027@gmail.com with the subject ParentEye legacy account deletion. Give the email address used for that account and state whether you request deletion of the parent account, a child account you manage, or both. We will verify your authority before processing and explain any data that must be retained for legal or security reasons. Do not send passwords, PINs, sharing links or identity documents in an ordinary email.

Request legacy account deletion

This page distinguishes the current account-free app from earlier account-based releases. Clearing local app storage does not itself delete an earlier cloud account.
