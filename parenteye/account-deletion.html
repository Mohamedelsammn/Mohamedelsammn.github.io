Delete your ParentEye account and data

ParentEye (com.parenteye.app) · 2creative.apps · Last updated 20 November 2026

English

You may request deletion of your ParentEye account and associated personal data without needing access to the app. This page is for both parent and child accounts. A parent or legal guardian should request deletion for a child account they manage.

How to request deletion

  1. Email dr.abdellrahman2027@gmail.com with the subject ParentEye account deletion request.
  2. State the email address used for the ParentEye account and whether you request deletion of the parent account, a child account you manage, or both. If you can no longer access that email, explain this in your message.
  3. We will verify that you control the account or are an authorized guardian before processing the request and will contact you if essential details are missing. Never email a password, PIN, pairing code, or identity document unless a secure verification process is provided.

Start deletion request by email

What the request covers

After verification, the request covers the relevant Firebase Authentication account and associated ParentEye profile, device pairings, pairing tokens, session metadata, activity records, and notification token, where attributable to that account. If deleting a parent account affects linked child accounts or active sharing, we will clarify the scope before processing. Removing the app from a device alone does not delete cloud data.

Possible retained data

Some information may be retained only when required for legal obligations, security or fraud prevention, or within temporary provider backups until those backups expire. We will explain any such retention in our response. Deleting an account ends access to its ParentEye services. Review the Privacy Policy for more detail.

This is a request route, not an automatic deletion form. Do not send account credentials by email.
