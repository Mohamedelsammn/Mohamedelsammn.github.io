Privacy Policy

ParentEye (com.parenteye.app) · Published by 2creative.apps · Last updated 20 November 2026

English

ParentEye is a parental-control application for a parent or guardian and their child's paired device. This policy explains the information the app handles, including information processed by third-party services. The parent or guardian is responsible for setting up and using the service lawfully and informing the child about monitoring.

Information we handle

  • Accounts: email address, display name, Firebase user ID, account role, and authentication information managed by Firebase Authentication. ParentEye does not need your account password in its own database.
  • Child devices and pairing: child account identifiers and names, pairing codes and status, device name/model, presence, battery level, selected app controls, and the parent–child device relationship.
  • Live viewing: session requests, approval/status, timestamps and duration, technical connection data needed for WebRTC signaling, and activity records. During an approved session, the child's screen is transmitted to the paired parent's device. WebRTC may use relay infrastructure when a direct connection is unavailable. The app does not intentionally save a recording of the screen stream as part of its normal live-view feature.
  • Notifications: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token and related device/platform information to deliver or wake the child device for session requests.
  • Local settings: language, role, consent acknowledgement, app-lock settings, and ad cooldown. The app PIN is held in encrypted local device storage; the current app implementation does not upload that PIN to Firestore.
  • Advertising: Google Mobile Ads may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers where available, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. ParentEye displays banner ads in parent and child home areas, rewarded ads when the parent voluntarily unlocks an additional device slot, an interstitial after a parent ends a live session, and eligible app-open ads for authenticated parents.

Why we use this information

We use account and device data to authenticate users, pair devices, manage child controls, deliver session requests, provide live viewing, show activity history, prevent misuse, and maintain the service. Advertising data is processed to display, measure and protect ads. Optional permissions are requested when the related feature is used.

Permissions and child-side disclosure

The parent may grant camera access to scan a pairing QR code. The child device may grant screen-capture, foreground-service, notification and background-operation permissions to support live sharing. The child app presents a screen-sharing disclosure and an ongoing notification while sharing. Screen viewing is intended only for a paired child device and must be used with the child informed and with any consent required by law.

Service providers and sharing

Google Firebase processes authentication, Firestore data, Remote Config and cloud messaging. Google AdMob processes advertising data as described in Google's Privacy Policy and advertising information. Connection and hosting providers may process traffic needed for signaling and relaying live sessions. We do not offer a feature to sell a child's screen recordings. Data may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect users and the service. Provider processing and any legal definition of “sharing” should be reflected accurately in the Google Play Data safety declaration.

Storage, security and retention

Account, pairing, activity and session metadata may be stored in Firebase or service infrastructure. Some settings and the app PIN stay on the device. We use access controls, encrypted transport where supported by the underlying services, and device-protected storage for local secrets. No system can guarantee absolute security. We retain data while needed to operate the account and service, and then delete or de-identify it when no longer needed or after a verified deletion request, subject to legally required retention and provider backup cycles. We do not promise a fixed retention period that the current implementation cannot verify.

Children and advertising

ParentEye is designed for parental supervision of children, not covert monitoring of adults. A parent or guardian must manage the child account and supervise use. The current child home screen includes a banner ad. If the app is offered to children in a region with additional consent or ad restrictions, those requirements must be met before serving ads there; this policy alone does not provide consent.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline permissions, stop a live session, or stop using the app, though some features may then be unavailable. For access, correction or deletion requests, email dr.abdellrahman2027@gmail.com. See the account-deletion instructions. Do not send passwords or PINs by email.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. The date above identifies the current version. Contact 2creative.apps at dr.abdellrahman2027@gmail.com with privacy questions.

سياسة الخصوصية

البيانات التي نتعامل معها

  • الحساب: البريد الإلكتروني والاسم ومعرّف حساب Firebase والدور، ومعلومات تسجيل الدخول التي تديرها Firebase Authentication. لا يحتاج تطبيق ParentEye إلى حفظ كلمة مرور الحساب في قاعدة بياناته الخاصة.
  • الأجهزة والاقتران: اسم الطفل ومعرّفه ورموز الاقتران وحالته واسم الجهاز وطرازه وحالة اتصاله ومستوى البطارية وإعدادات التحكم بالتطبيقات والعلاقة بين جهاز الطفل وولي الأمر.
  • المشاهدة المباشرة: طلبات الجلسات والموافقة وحالتها ووقتها ومدتها وبيانات الاتصال التقنية اللازمة لـWebRTC وسجل النشاط. تُنقل شاشة الطفل أثناء الجلسة المعتمدة إلى جهاز ولي الأمر المقترن، وقد يُستخدم خادم ترحيل إذا تعذر الاتصال المباشر. لا يحفظ التطبيق تسجيلًا للشاشة عمدًا ضمن ميزة المشاهدة المباشرة المعتادة.
  • الإشعارات: رمز Firebase Cloud Messaging وبعض معلومات الجهاز لإيصال طلبات الجلسات إلى جهاز الطفل.
  • الإعدادات المحلية: اللغة والدور وإقرار الإفصاح والقفل ووقت آخر إعلان. يُحفظ رمز PIN الخاص بالتطبيق في مساحة تخزين محلية مشفرة، ولا يرفعه التنفيذ الحالي إلى Firestore.
  • الإعلانات: قد تعالج Google Mobile Ads عنوان IP ومعرّفات الجهاز أو الإعلان عند توفرها والتفاعل مع الإعلانات ومعلومات التشخيص. يظهر إعلان بانر في واجهة المنزل لولي الأمر والطفل، وإعلان بمكافأة يختاره ولي الأمر لإضافة جهاز، وإعلان بعد إنهاء جلسة مشاهدة مباشرة من ولي الأمر، وإعلان عند فتح التطبيق لبعض أولياء الأمور المسجلين.

أسباب الاستخدام والأذونات

نستخدم البيانات لتسجيل الدخول وإقران الأجهزة وإدارة إعدادات الطفل وإرسال طلبات الجلسات وتقديم المشاهدة المباشرة وسجل النشاط وحماية الخدمة. تُستخدم بيانات الإعلان لعرض الإعلانات وقياسها ومكافحة إساءة الاستخدام. قد يُطلب إذن الكاميرا لمسح QR، وأذونات التقاط الشاشة والخدمة الأمامية والإشعارات والعمل في الخلفية لتشغيل مشاركة شاشة الطفل. يعرض تطبيق الطفل إفصاحًا عن المشاركة وإشعارًا مستمرًا أثناءها.

مقدمو الخدمة والمشاركة
